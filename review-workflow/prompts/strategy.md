# Creative strategy and portfolio architecture

Review efficacy: audience, promise, distinctive character, navigation of the
bible, hierarchy of rules versus examples, usability for a designer or engineer,
and a coherent parent/product brand relationship. Does the system make sensible
choices easy to reproduce?

Check that FireFlow, Unfazed.dev, Lanni, and PrivateInference (PI) each have separate
and usable design specifications. PI must use a bold mathematical pi symbol.
There are five requested product slots but only four explicit names: verify the
fifth is supported by supplied evidence or transparently marked as an assumption;
never silently invent a fifth business. Parent Persistent Labs is not itself a
fifth sub-product. Check all proposed claims against dated source context.

Product specs should cover positioning, tone, mark and lockup, colors with usage,
typography, spacing and shape, motion, UI examples and relationship to the parent.
Challenge unverified promises about security, privacy, speed or availability.

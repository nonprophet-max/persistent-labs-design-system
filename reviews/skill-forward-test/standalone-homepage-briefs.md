# Homepage briefs from isolated product packages

Both briefs use only the corresponding standalone skill folder copied under `isolated/`. No parent package, external service or live website was needed. Statements reflect the supplied references; a future live homepage still needs current evidence for particular capability and availability claims.

## FireFlow

Build a homepage for technical teams creating agent experiences. Use proposed headline **“Make every step count.”** and descriptor **“Orchestration, persistent memory & chat UX.”** Explain one FireFlow product with three parts: the orchestration engine, MemoryTree for persistent memory and FlameChorus for chat UX. Lead with one primary **“Inspect the workflow”** action, linked to an actual workflow explanation when built.

The hero leads with FireFlow in Red Hat Display 700, optionally accompanied by the supplied proposed schematic F. It is not a replacement for an approved original italic logotype. Place “by Persistent Labs” separately and quietly. Use graphite `#1C1E21`, white, orange `#F7931A`, deep orange `#8C4900` for text on white and tint `#FFF0DE`. Orange actions use graphite labels, hover `#FFA638`, pressed `#E88712`.

Organize the body around an illustrative run: received input, awaiting review, execution not started. Explain the engine, a memory entry showing source/scope/update time, and the conversation’s proposed action. Sky blue identifies MemoryTree and warm gold identifies FlameChorus inside one FireFlow shell. Neither component gets a product card, independent endorsement or competing primary action color. Label sample run information illustrative, use textual states and avoid continuous execution animation.

Use 24px marketing cards, 8px workflow nodes, 2px connectors, 12px ports and 24px step spacing. Flow left to right at wide sizes, then stack with explicit arrows. Use 14px logs at 1.6 leading and Red Hat Mono for identifiers; body starts at 16px. Controls have 44px minimum hit areas, visible focus and reduced-motion behavior. Motion is a restrained 200ms transition tied to an actual change.

**Standalone resources:** `SKILL.md`, `references/brand.md`, `references/foundations.md`, `references/design-tokens.json`, `references/assets.md`, `assets/tokens.css`, `assets/fonts.css`, three WOFF2 font files and licenses, plus `assets/fireflow.svg`, `assets/memorytree.svg` and `assets/persistent-labs.svg`. Text wordmarks are typeset from bundled fonts; vectors are symbols. No FlameChorus symbol is required or promised. Exactly-once execution, limitless memory and licensing claims require verification before use.

## Galactica

Build a proposed family homepage treatment for the live privacy technology firm spanning AI and blockchain. Use proposed headline **“Privacy. With purpose.”** and descriptor **“Privacy technology across AI & blockchain.”** Maintain separate AI and blockchain editorial lanes; explain specific services only when evidence is available. Keep token-market material outside the central firm narrative.

Use `assets/galactica.svg` as the complete **Galactica.com** lockup, minimum 160px wide and one capital-height clear space. Preserve bold orange dot `#F7931A`; it stays orange on light and dark grounds and never acts alone. Lettering is outlined, so the logo does not depend on local fonts. This owner-directed family adaptation is not an extracted live-site master.

Ground the page in graphite `#1C1E21`, white and restrained monochrome imagery. Proposed copper `#F4A77A` marks relevant paths on dark surfaces; `#97502A` is text on white; `#FFF1E8` supplies a light tint. Filled copper actions carry graphite labels, hover `#FFC29D`, pressed `#E3986D`. These copper UI colors differ deliberately from the logo’s orange dot.

An illustrative disclosure section can pair **what a service requests** with **what would be disclosed**, using the example age statement without presenting an actual generated proof. Name requester, purpose, requested statement, disclosed fields and limits. Show “Awaiting review — example,” “Not generated” and “No authorization given.” A proposed **“Review the disclosure”** control should open the example explanation rather than imply a live proof service. Do not infer privacy guarantees, retention promises, simulated verification or speculative AI capabilities from the diagram.

Use Red Hat Display 600/700, Text 400/500 and Mono only for certificate identifiers. Body is 16px/1.6, labels 14px/1.5. Use 24px enclosing panels, 12px groups, 8px controls, 1px visible boundaries and 20px mobile gutters. Stack request then disclosure on mobile. Selection transitions take 150–200ms with reduced-motion support; avoid orbits and decorative scanning. Schematic lines work without photography; human imagery is not bundled.

**Standalone resources:** `SKILL.md`, `references/brand.md`, `references/foundations.md`, `references/design-tokens.json`, `references/assets.md`, `assets/tokens.css`, `assets/fonts.css`, three WOFF2 fonts and licenses, plus `assets/galactica.svg` and `assets/persistent-labs.svg`. All required identity assets are present. Specific live capabilities and imagery need separate evidence/assets if added later.

These are written briefs. No rendered homepage was produced, so this test does not claim responsive, visual, browser-font, contrast or interaction verification of an implementation.

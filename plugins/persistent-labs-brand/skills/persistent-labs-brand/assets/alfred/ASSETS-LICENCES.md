# Alfred asset provenance and licences

The logos, marks and both butler images are owner-supplied assets from the Alfred v0.3 handoff. No general ownership, trademark or redistribution rights are granted. The owner chose to use the mascot despite the recorded resemblance concern; this is not legal clearance. Raster linework is preserved and is not pixel-exact to the ink token.

Instrument Serif, Cormorant Garamond and Outfit retain the SIL Open Font License. See `fonts/instrumentserif-OFL.txt`, `fonts/cormorantgaramond-OFL.txt` and `fonts/outfit-OFL.txt`. The Outfit Latin-ext file was mislabeled Cyrillic in the handoff; its bytes are unchanged. Outfit Cyrillic body text uses system-ui fallback. See `provenance.json` for original paths and hashes.

01Foundations

Principles

Persistent Labs sells deterministic, regulated infrastructure for AI agents. The brand has to feel calm and engineered, but warm enough that people trust it with money. Across every surface that comes down to the same pairing: a near-black graphite canvas, one warm gold, heavy geometric headlines and a lot of air.

1. Graphite first, gold second

The default ground is ink #1C1E21. Gold #FBD78E marks the single most important thing in view: the primary action, a highlighted phrase or a live state. When gold is everywhere, nothing is important.

2. Engineered, not ornamental

Show the machinery: real flow graphs, execution logs, typed ports, audit trails. Proof beats metaphor. Diagrams and product screenshots count as primary imagery, not decoration.

3. Heavy headlines, quiet body

Headlines are Red Hat Display Bold with tight tracking (−3%) and tight leading (1.04–1.05). Body copy steps down to white at 50–75% opacity, so the hierarchy comes from contrast and weight rather than color.

4. Soft geometry

Large radii (30–40 px on cards), fully rounded pills for every button and tag, and no hard corners on containers. The softness balances the density of the technical content.

5. Light as a material

Depth comes from glows, not drop shadows: 200 px gold or aurora blurs, prismatic hero footage, and gradient borders that shift slowly. Keep glows to one or two per viewport.

6. Honest comparison

Green, yellow and red are reserved for truthful capability ratings, including FireFlow's own gaps. Semantic color never doubles as decoration.

Logo, lockups & naming

The mark is a looped “∞/X” ribbon made of four strokes, rendered at about 50% opacity in ink or white so it reads as a watermark-like signature. Logo rules

Do

  • Keep clear space equal to the mark's height on every side.
  • Render the mark in ink #1C1E21 or white at 50–60% opacity. On the website header it animates a gold #FBD78E shine sweep.
  • Size the web header lockup at about 60 px mark width (3.125 vw at 1920) plus the 154 px wordmark.
  • In co-brand lockups, separate the two brands with a 1 px vertical ink rule.

Don't

  • Recolor the mark gold, or fill it with a gradient (the shine is a transient effect only).
  • Set the wordmark in a weight other than Medium, or letter-space it.
  • Put the mark on busy imagery without a solid or glass container.
  • Stack the wordmark under the mark. The lockup is horizontal only.

Typesetting rules

Do

  • Track headlines at −3% (for example, −0.1vw on 3.333vw). Track body at −2%.
  • Emphasize one phrase per headline with gold, or with Bold inside Regular body.
  • Keep body line length at 55–70 characters. Card copy maxes out near 36–45 characters.
  • Use weight 300 only for large numerals and the occasional lead.
  • Set sentence case everywhere. Reserve CAPS for eyebrows and tiny labels.

Don't

  • Use Inter, Aeonik Pro or system fonts in brand work.
  • Set Red Hat Text for headlines. It is for controls only.
  • Use italics for emphasis. Italic is reserved for case-study captions and the “fireflow” logotype.
  • Center long paragraphs. Only centered section titles and short CTA copy are allowed.
  • Go below 12 px in print or 14 px on screen.

Layout & grid

The website is a fluid proportional canvas rather than a column grid. Every dimension is a vw fraction of the artboard, so a composition keeps exactly the same proportions from 1280 to 2560 px. Below 920 px the layout switches to a 420 px mobile artboard and stacks into a single column.

Artboards

1920 desktop · 420 mobile

Convert with vw = px / 19.2 on desktop and vw = px / 4.2 on mobile. There is one breakpoint: max-width: 920px.

Containers

Header

Floating glass pill

Composition patterns

Hero, left-weighted

Copy column about 50vw wide, inset 10.4vw from the container edge, 16.6vw from the top. Full-bleed prismatic video or 3D object behind it on the right. Social icons sit above the H1.

Hero, centered (product)

Centered display-xl headline with a gold span, 59vw subtitle, and two 280×52 pills side by side with a 12 px gap. The hero fills at least 100svh minus 200 px.

Section title + card row

Split feature (alternating)

Screenshot (727 px) on one side, a 653 px text column on the other, and the order flips on every other row. Product eyebrow sits in the product's accent color with a dot. Rows are 160 px apart.

A full-width Paper #F6F6F6 panel (72 px radius) wrapped in the animated aurora ring. It holds accordion rows for dense “what it does / why it matters” content.

10Application

Imagery & iconography

Hero imagery: prismatic glass

Dark, soft-focus footage of glass monoliths and prisms splitting light into thin spectral edges (sphere.mp4, the Lanni hero). The palette stays near-monochrome, with rainbow only at the refracted edges. Blend with mix-blend-mode: lighten or screen at 50%.

Product proof: real UI

Real screenshots of the flow editor (typed-port node graph with colored wires), the VFS and executions panels, and the chat app. Frame them in macOS-style windows at radius 12–18 with a 1 px white-20% border. Don't use fake dashboards.

3D glyph tiles

Iridescent chrome or glass icons (shield, coin, padlock) on dark rounded tiles with a cyan-to-lilac rim light, used on feature carousel cards.

Line illustrations

Isometric outline drawings (cube, ring, arrow) with one flat color block (lime, gold or coral). They sit in the top-right corner of Paper stat cards and bleed off the edge.

UI icons

Outline icons in the 1.5 px stroke Lucide/Tabler style at 16–24 px, filled in ink on light and white on dark. Social icons are round (circle plus glyph) and invert on hover.

Maps & data

Dark map tiles, a gold route line, and red star pins with white rings. For charts use Plotly (supported in chat) restyled to the palette: gold first series, then sky and orange, with grid at white 10%.

11Application

Voice & copy

The voice is confident and specific, written for risk owners. It names the failure mode and then the guarantee. It uses short declaratives and contrast pairs.

Patterns that work

• Contrast pairs: “Don't become a node / Own the rails.” “Most agents answer. Lanni executes.”
• Numbers as proof: “$5m+”, “2–4 years”, “25+ event types”, always with a source in parentheses.
• Mechanism named: “DBOS persists every step before proceeding.”
• One gold phrase per headline: “Agents that do more”.

House style

• Sentence case for headings. Title Case only on buttons (“Schedule a Demo”). Pick one and keep it; sentence case is recommended.
• Use an en dash for ranges (2–4 years) and an em dash for asides. Never use a spaced hyphen as a dash.
• Say “agents”, “flows”, “nodes” and “rooms”, which are the product nouns.
• Keep it regulatory-grade: no hype adjectives, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
• Use British or US spelling consistently. The site mixes “specialised” with “organization”.

What it establishes for the system

A light mode for collateral

The PDF puts the brand on a white-to-dawn gradient (#FFFFFF → #FDEED2 → #FAE3B6) inside a 26 pt ink frame #1D1E22. That gives the dark-first system a sanctioned warm-light variant without inventing new colors.

The same type system

The glyphs are Red Hat Display throughout (embedded as Type 3 outlines). The scale uses heavy 700 headlines with −3% tracking, and body at 300–400 in ink at about 70–80%, which matches the website.

Gold as a surface

Gold appears as a fill for featured cards (#FDD687 ≈ brand #FBD78E), highlight chips (“no other open-source solutions”) and the “2–4 years.” pill (gold text on an ink capsule, 12:1).

Status-chip language

“in production” (gold chip), “part of the stack” (ink chip with a code icon) and “in development” (muted chip on a faded card) form a clear maturity vocabulary. The bible adopts it as the Status component (§08).

The aurora, printed

The product panel on p.2 is a mesh gradient of teal #365D6E, olive #868E5A, coral #E88F67 and cream. It is the same family as the website's animated aurora ring (#3E859E · #96AB70 · #E28B5F · #FBD78E).

Dark “argument bands”

Each page closes on an ink band (r≈30) with a gold headline and outlined sub-cards (“Why a bank should not go down this path alone”, “Useful Links”). It is a strong, repeatable closing device.

Page by page

p.1 · Narrative

“Built for the moments where a text reply isn't enough”

A two-column layout (about 55/45). The left column is the story in alternating white and cream bands with gold bullet dots and a gold gradient pull-quote card with the mark. The right column holds four dark app screenshots (Lisbon, hike, anime room, idle cash) with italic underlined captions that link to Figma prototypes. It is the strongest page, because it shows the product instead of describing it.

p.2 · What is FireFlow

Products + feature table

An H1 plus a bold lead, bullet focus areas, the aurora product panel with three products in white text, a gold highlight chip, then an eyebrow (“PLATFORM EVOLUTION”) and a large H1 above an eight-row feature table whose “without it” column is tinted red. The logic is clear, but the page is text-heavy.

p.3 · Production-grade

Staggered status cards + stages

Four cards at stepped heights (a staircase that suggests maturity): two dark, one gold, one faded. Then a two-line headline with the gold-on-ink “2–4 years.” pill, a five-row stage table, and the dark argument band with Gartner and Knight Capital proof points. This is the best-composed page.

p.4 · Market comparison

22-row comparison matrix

Five columns: FireFlow + Flame Chorus against LangFlow + Temporal, n8n and Claude Code. The cells are tone-coded green, yellow and red, with a dark header and a “Useful Links” band. It is dense and informative, but the least legible page in the document.

Open decisions for your review

The sources disagree in a few places. The bible currently follows the recommendation on each line. Please confirm or overrule these before this becomes a skill.

1. Company nameResolved: Persistent Labs, which replaces both “Persistent AI” (logo, ©) and “PersistentAI” (docs, Lanni copy). The logo wordmark, site footer, © line and docs all need updating.
2. Memory product nameMemoryTree (site) vs Memory Tree (docs) vs Memory / Memory Agent / Memory system (PDF). Recommended: MemoryTree.
3. Gold hover value#FFCB63 (site) vs #FFCC33 (docs). Recommended: #FFCB63, which is softer and closer to the base gold.
4. App primary goldThe app's --color-primary #EABD64 vs brand #FBD78E. Recommended: keep #EABD64 only as a pressed/active state and use #FBD78E as primary.
5. Light-mode website?The marketing site is dark-only. Docs and the PDF prove a light mode. Recommended: keep web dark-only, with light reserved for docs and collateral.
6. Button casing“Schedule a Demo” (Title) vs “Get in touch” (sentence) on the same header. Recommended: sentence case everywhere.
7. Node count146+, ~300 or 320+. The design system can't settle this, so you need to supply the correct figure.
8. Legacy fontsAeonik Pro (app) and Inter (docs default) are both still loaded. Recommended: remove both and use Red Hat only.

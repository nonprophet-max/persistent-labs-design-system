# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People and AI tools creating material for Persistent Labs and its product brands. They need to find current identity rules, inspect useful examples, and obtain the exact assets and specifications for a task.

## Product Purpose

The central brand library presents the parent system and distinct product identities as a shareable reference. The same maintained source material supplies portable brand skills and plain chatbot guides.

## Operating Context

This is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript library generated with Python's standard library. The source is the public `nonprophet-max/persistent-labs-design-system` repository. Max requested moving the complete central walkthrough from Netlify to GitHub Pages during the Alfred addition.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product chapters preserve each identity within the shared library navigation.
- Canonical product and token sources generate the displayed bible and reusable packages.
- Offline bundled assets and font licences travel with the relevant package.
- The library defines brand application. Illustrations and specimens do not establish product capabilities, availability or partnerships.
- Existing source archives remain unchanged; historic review records apply only to their recorded versions.

## Brand Commitments

Persistent Labs retains its established parent identity. FireFlow, Unfazed.dev, Lanni, Private Inference, Galactica and Alfred retain their product-specific systems. MemoryTree and FlameChorus remain parts of FireFlow. Alfred's supplied v0.3 identity is the basis of the current addition; its design brief is approved.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing canonical sources, original marks, Pi's supplied source PDF, bundled fonts/licences and prior review records are in the repository. Alfred's owner-supplied v0.3 handoff supplies its assets and identity rules. Report v5 is unavailable; Max explicitly instructed us to develop from the existing assets while it is missing.

## Product Principles

- Make current rules easy to find and reproduce.
- Keep distinct identities distinct.
- Share one authority between human and AI editions.
- Demonstrate rules with real assets and usable examples.
- State evidence and verification limits accurately.
